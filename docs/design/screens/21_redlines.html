Responsive behavior
≥ 960px
Full layout. Sidebar expanded at 228px (icon + label). Budget grid 3 columns. Hero summary in one row.
720–960
Sidebar collapses to a 60px icon rail (labels hidden, active tint kept; tooltips on hover). Budget grid 2 columns. Hero net stays large; income/spent move below it.
< 720px
720px is the minimum window. Below it, budget grid drops to 1 column and the Quick-Add modal goes full-width (16px inset). No mobile/tablet layout in current scope.
Confirmed decisions
· Min window 720px; no tablet layout.
· Alert banners are persistent until the budget situation resolves (not dismissible).
· Quick-Add success → toast slides in, modal auto-closes; newly added row appears optimistically.
· Currency USD $, tabular figures, right-aligned amounts.
· Motion 150–200ms; respects prefers-reduced-motion.
· Status never relies on color alone — paired with %, icon & label.
